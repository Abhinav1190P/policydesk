# Globex Manufacturing Shift and Overtime Policy

## Shift timings

The plants run three shifts: Shift A from 6:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Shift B from 2:00 PM to 10:00 PM, and Shift C (night shift) from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.

## Shift differential

Employees working Shift C (night shift) receive a shift differential of 10% of their basic pay for every night shift worked. Employees working Shift B receive a shift differential of 5% of basic pay.

## Overtime

Overtime must be approved in advance by the shift supervisor. Overtime is paid at twice the normal hourly rate. No employee may work more than 50 hours of overtime in a calendar quarter. Overtime on a public holiday is paid at three times the normal hourly rate.

## Shift swaps

Employees may swap shifts with a colleague of the same skill level with the shift supervisor's approval, at least 48 hours before the shift starts.

## Rest periods

There must be at least 12 hours between the end of one shift and the start of the next for the same employee.
