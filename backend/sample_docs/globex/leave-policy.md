# Globex Manufacturing Leave Policy

Applies to all permanent employees at the Pune and Chakan plants and the Pune head office.

## Annual leave

Permanent employees get 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried forward into the next year; the rest lapses on 31 December.

Shift employees must apply for annual leave at least 14 days in advance, and the leave must be approved by their shift supervisor so the line stays staffed. Office employees apply at least 5 days in advance in the SAP SuccessFactors portal.

## Sick leave

Employees get 7 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave of 2 or more consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Casual leave

Employees get 3 days of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave and cannot be carried forward.

## Plant shutdown

The plants close for an annual maintenance shutdown during the first week of November. Shift employees must take 4 days of their annual leave during the shutdown.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents get 10 days of paid paternity leave within 3 months of the birth.
