# Globex Manufacturing Travel and Expense Policy

## Travel mode

For journeys under 8 hours, employees travel by train in AC 2-tier or by company car. Flights are allowed in economy class only for journeys over 8 hours by train, or with a general manager's approval.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are capped at Rs 5,000 per night in all cities. Employees should use the company's partner hotels where available.

## Daily allowance

Employees on business travel get a daily allowance of Rs 1,500 per day for meals and local travel within India. International travel requires director approval and the allowance is set case by case.

## Claims

Submit travel claims in SAP Concur within 15 days of returning. Original bills are required for all hotel and travel expenses. Claims are reimbursed within 10 working days of approval.
