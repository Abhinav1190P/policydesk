# Globex Manufacturing IT and Plant Safety Policy

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 12 characters and must be changed every 90 days. Accounts lock after 5 failed login attempts; call the IT helpdesk to unlock.

## IT helpdesk

The IT helpdesk is reachable at extension 4400 or it-support@globex.example, 24 hours a day for plant systems. Raise tickets for any laptop, printer, network or access problems. High priority tickets for plant systems get a response within 1 hour.

## USB and removable media

USB storage drives are blocked on all company computers. To move files, use the approved Globex OneDrive.

## Personal devices

Personal phones may not be used on the production floor except in designated zones. Company email can be used on personal phones only with the Intune app installed.

## Plant safety

Safety shoes, a helmet, and safety glasses must be worn at all times on the production floor. Visitors must be escorted and wear a visitor badge. Report every safety incident or near miss to your shift supervisor before the end of the shift.
