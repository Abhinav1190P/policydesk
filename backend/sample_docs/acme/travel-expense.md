# Acme Cloud Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking travel

All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk, Navan. Flights of under 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are capped at Rs 7,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) and Rs 4,500 per night in other Indian cities. International hotel caps are listed in Navan for each city.

## Per diem

Domestic travel gets a per diem of Rs 2,000 per day for meals and local transport. International travel gets a per diem of USD 60 per day. Per diem is not paid for days when meals are provided by a client or conference.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports in Expensify within 30 days of the expense. Attach receipts for every expense above Rs 500. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment, minibar charges, and upgrades you choose yourself are not reimbursed.

## Client entertainment

Client meals need your manager's pre-approval and are capped at Rs 3,000 per person.
