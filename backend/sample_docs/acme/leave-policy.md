# Acme Cloud Leave Policy

Effective from 1 April 2026. Applies to all full-time employees of Acme Cloud in India.

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service. New joiners get leave pro-rated from their joining date.

You can carry forward up to 8 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Any balance above 8 days lapses on 31 December. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March of the next year.

Leave of more than 3 consecutive working days must be applied for in the HR portal (PeopleHub) at least 7 days in advance and approved by your manager.

## Sick leave

Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry forward. For sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days, upload a doctor's certificate to PeopleHub within 5 days of returning to work.

## Public holidays

Acme Cloud observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published in PeopleHub every December. If you work on a public holiday at your manager's request, you get one compensatory day off, to be used within 60 days.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents get 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption. Adoptive parents of a child under 3 months get 12 weeks of paid leave.

## Bereavement leave

Employees get up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member.

## Leave without pay

Leave without pay of up to 30 days can be granted only after all annual leave is used, with approval from your manager and the HR business partner.

Email people@acmecloud.example for leave questions.
