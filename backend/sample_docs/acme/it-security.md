# Acme Cloud IT and Security Policy

## Accounts and passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) with the Okta Verify app is required for all company accounts. Never share your password or MFA codes with anyone, including IT staff.

## Laptops and devices

Every employee gets an Acme-issued laptop with full disk encryption enabled. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Install software only from the Self Service app; anything not listed there needs an IT ticket for approval.

Report a lost or stolen device within 2 hours to it-help@acmecloud.example or on the IT help line +91 80 4000 1234, so the device can be locked and wiped remotely.

## Network access

The GlobalProtect VPN is required to reach internal systems and production environments. Production access is granted for 8 hours at a time through the access portal and needs a ticket with a reason.

## Email and data

Do not forward work documents to personal email or store them on personal cloud drives. Customer data must never be copied to removable media. Report suspected phishing with the Report Phish button in Outlook.

## Getting help

Raise IT problems by creating a ticket in the IT portal or through PolicyDesk. Hardware failures are fixed or replaced within 2 business days. The IT help desk works 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM IST on weekdays.
