# Acme Cloud Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule

Acme Cloud works on a hybrid model. Employees attached to the Bengaluru or Hyderabad offices are expected in the office 3 days a week, on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Teams may agree on different anchor days with their director's approval.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours where possible.

## Work from anywhere

Employees can work from any location in India for up to 4 weeks per calendar year, in addition to normal remote days. Working from outside India requires approval from HR and the security team at least 30 days in advance, because of tax and data rules. Working from outside India is limited to 2 weeks per year.

## Home office allowance

Employees receive an internet reimbursement of up to Rs 1,500 per month, claimed through the monthly expense report. New joiners get a one-time home office allowance of Rs 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor, claimable within the first 90 days.

## Security at home

Use only your Acme-issued laptop for work. Do not work from public Wi-Fi without the company VPN. Lock your screen whenever you step away.

## Fully remote roles

Fully remote roles are approved case by case by the department head and are listed as remote in the offer letter.
