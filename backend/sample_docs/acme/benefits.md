# Acme Cloud Benefits Guide

## Health insurance

Acme Cloud provides a family floater health insurance cover of Rs 5,00,000 per year for the employee, spouse and up to two children. Parents can be added with a premium paid by the employee. Group term life insurance covers 3 times annual fixed pay.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of Rs 50,000 per financial year for courses, certifications, books and conferences. Get your manager's approval before spending, then claim it in Expensify with the invoice.

## Wellness

Employees get a wellness allowance of Rs 1,000 per month for a gym, yoga class, or sports club membership. Free and confidential counselling sessions are available through the employee assistance program.

## Meals and commute

Lunch is provided free in the office on anchor days. Employees who come to the office can claim up to Rs 3,000 per month for commute costs.
